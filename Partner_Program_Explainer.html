Why it's hard the usual way

A stranger seeing an ad is a long way from choosing a school.

The cold way

"Here's an ad."

We pay to reach people who've never heard of us. Most scroll past; the few who click don't know us and rarely book. Expensive, and slow.

The warm way

"My studio loves this school."

A business the family already trusts invites them to something valuable — and Aspen is right there. We arrive pre-introduced and pre-trusted.

The whole idea, in one phrase

Borrowed trust.

A local business whose customers are already our ideal families has spent years earning their trust. We lean on that trust — they vouch for us to people who already believe in them.

And it runs both ways: when we promote them to our families, we lend them our trust in return. Two trusted brands, each opening their circle to the other.

Let's address the obvious question

"Why would a business ever do this?"

This is where everyone gets stuck. It sounds like we're asking a busy owner for a favor — to go promote a school to their own customers.

If that were the deal, of course they'd say no.
But that's not the deal.

The reframe that changes everything

We're not asking a favor. We're filling their house.

We host on their turf and put our promotion — including paid ads at the right tier — behind driving families to their door.

And it's not just a school doing it — it's Aspen Academy: home of the nation's first integrated PK–8 Leadership, Finance & Entrepreneurship program, the premier entrepreneurial school in North America. They align their brand with that, and watch us spend real dollars to bring families to their location.

We both want the exact same thing: a packed room at their place.

And the event itself is the value

A premium, custom-built workshop — free.

It's not a generic talk. We design the presentation around the host's world:

Kids' gym → an expert on physical movement & brain development in children.
Dance studio → confidence, focus, and the discipline-to-achievement link.
Pediatric dentist → raising resilient, responsible kids (Love & Logic).
$90–$200

what comparable expert parenting programs cost (Love & Logic's official course is $119/person).

Their families get real value, free — the partner looks generous and on-brand. The partner fills a room far faster with "free $119 workshop." Aspen meets ideal families before enrollment ever comes up.

The thing no other school can offer

Our kids fall in love with their business.

At the top tier, a partner joins the LiFE program: our students study their real business, build them a marketing campaign or video, and invite the owner in as a Shark Tank guest judge.

They walk away with real student-made work, a starring role on campus, and a story they'll tell forever — and every one of those families now knows their brand.

Connect it back to those empty seats

Every partner is aimed at a specific gap.

This isn't "general awareness." Each partner is chosen to reach the families in a grade we need to fill.

PreK · Kinder — 31 seats

The Little Gym · Kickin' It · My Gym · Goldfish Swim School · a Cherry Hills family photographer — full of families with 2–5 year olds.

6th · 7th — 41 seats

Dance studios (girls 10–13) · pediatric orthodontists (patients 10–14) · STEM programs · Spero Education — exactly our middle-grade families.

We've already scored a "Dream 20" list of real local partners, mapped to these gaps.
